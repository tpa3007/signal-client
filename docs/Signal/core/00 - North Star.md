# Signal - North Star

Signal is a personal prediction-market research lab for finding hidden gems on Polymarket.

The goal is not to build a fast trading bot. The goal is to build a disciplined research system that can:

- discover overlooked markets;
- reason from public evidence;
- record every forecast, including skipped ideas;
- learn from resolutions;
- measure calibration and edge honestly;
- turn repeated research into a body of work.

The project should become a private "research terminal" for Polymarket: part forecasting notebook, part evidence engine, part calibration lab, part execution journal.

## Core Belief

Hidden gems are more likely to appear where:

- the market is liquid enough to enter but not so large that every sophisticated desk is watching;
- the question depends on public but scattered information;
- the resolution horizon is long enough for research to matter;
- the crowd is anchored, bored, distracted, or overreacting;
- the edge survives spread, slippage, and ambiguity in the rules.

## What Signal Must Prove

Signal is only useful if it can answer these questions:

- Did our forecasts beat the market price?
- Were our 60%, 70%, 80% forecasts calibrated?
- Which verticals produce real edge after spread?
- Which sources and reasoning patterns helped?
- Which confidence levels were overconfident?
- Which markets looked attractive but were actually traps?
- Did the system improve over time?

## Operating Principle

No invisible decisions.

Every market analysis should leave a trace:

- what was seen;
- what was believed;
- what evidence mattered;
- why a signal was or was not emitted;
- what happened after resolution.

This is what makes the project a research lab instead of a diary of lucky calls.
