# Signal - Moonshot Scout

Moonshot Scout is the controlled-enthusiasm layer.

It exists because some hidden gems start as a feeling:

> "This is cheap, weird, time-sensitive, and maybe the market is asleep."

The project should not suppress that instinct. It should capture it, score it, and force it through filters before it becomes a signal.

## What Moonshot Is

Moonshot is not a bet recommendation.

It is a first-pass research intake for high-payout ideas that are risky but not random.

Good moonshots usually have:

- low entry price;
- large payout multiple;
- real catalyst;
- identifiable mechanism;
- non-zero evidence;
- clear resolution path;
- acceptable liquidity/spread;
- explicit kill criteria.

## What Moonshot Rejects

Moonshot should reject:

- "it is cheap, so why not" ideas;
- vibes without mechanism;
- impossible timelines;
- markets where resolution wording destroys the thesis;
- illiquid contracts where spread eats the entire edge;
- social-media hype with no source trail.

## Tools

- `record_moonshot_review`
- `moonshot_watchlist`

## Score Components

- probability edge;
- payout multiple;
- catalyst strength;
- mechanism clarity;
- evidence quality;
- resolution clarity;
- liquidity;
- spread;
- narrative heat;
- anti-random score.

The anti-random score is important. It asks:

> "Is this risky in an intelligent way, or just gambling dressed up as research?"

## Example Patterns

Gemini reasoning flagship at `0.05`:

- cheap optionality;
- high-attention catalyst;
- narrow wording risk;
- good Moonshot candidate, not a high-confidence forecast.

Knesset dissolved by May 31 at `0.13`:

- political/procedural catalyst;
- actor incentive mismatch;
- short timeline risk;
- good watchlist Moonshot, but requires procedural tracking.

## Workflow

1. Moonshot Scout catches a risky but structured idea.
2. Deep research checks sources, actors, mechanisms, and resolution wording.
3. Hidden-gem review decides whether it deserves priority.
4. Normal signal logic decides whether executable edge survives spread/confidence.
5. Quality backtest later studies whether the market repriced toward the thesis.

This keeps the project imaginative without becoming careless.
